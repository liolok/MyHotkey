# Features

### General

- Drop Lantern (or Light Bulb if no Lantern)
- Open/Close Polar Bearger Bin
- Walking Cane: Equip a speed-up hand tool, or switch back to former hand equipment.
  - (Priority: Walking Cane > The Lazy Explorer > King's Staff > Charged Elding Spear > Wooden Walking Stick > Thulecite Club > Speedy Balloon)
- Equip / Use Snurtle Shell Armor
- Jump In / Travel via: Wormhole, Big Slimy Pit, Time Rift, Sinkhole, Stairs
- Steer/Stop
- Lower/Raise Anchor
- Open/Close Umbralla: If no Umbralla on ground, will try to drop one and open.
- Dance (For Wendy and Maxwell players)
- Murder (Will ignore Scorching Sunfish & Ice Bream)
- Read (Preserve one last usage, also balance durability in Bookcase.)
- Batch Move
- Click Container Button: For example Cook/Wrap/Destroy
- Save Game (press twice or hold to confirm action)
- Reset Game: Restart the server from the last save. (press twice or hold to confirm action)
- Toggle Movement Prediction

### Willow

- Lighter: Equip Lighter, or switch back to former hand equipment.
- Absorb Fire or Stop: Equip Lighter and "Absorb Fire", or "Stop Absorbing Fire".
- Drop Bernie
- Flame Cast (fast/manual cast)
- Combustion (fast cast)
- Fire Ball (fast/manual cast)
- Burning Frenzy (fast cast)
- Lunar Flame (fast cast)
- Shadow Fire (fast cast)

### Wolfgang

- Dumbbell: Equip Dumbbell, or switch back to former hand equipment.
- Lift Dumbbell or Stop: Equip Dumbbell and "Lift", or "Stop Lifting".
  - (Priority: Gembell > Marbell > Golden Dumbbell > Dumbbell > Icebell > Firebell > Thermbell)

### Wendy

- Walk to Pipspook's lost toy

### Abigail

- Apply Spectral Cure-All (Picnic Casket Supported)
- Summon / Recall (fast cast)
- Rile Up / Soothe (fast cast)
- Escape (fast cast)
- Attack At (fast cast)
- Haunt At (fast cast)
- Scare (fast cast)

### WX-78

- Initiate/Terminate Sonic Waves
- Activate/Deactivate Block

### Woodie

- Kitschy Idol: Find first idol in inventory bar then backpack, unequip all except backpack then eat.

### Maxwell

- Use Magician's Top Hat, or Stop.
- Shadow Servant (fast/manual cast)
- Shadow Duelist (fast/manual cast)
- Shadow Sneak (fast/manual cast)
- Shadow Prison (fast/manual cast)
- Attack Shadow Puppet

### Wigfrid

- Sing Battle Call (Battle Call Canister Supported)
- Lightning Strike / Block (Dash up to 8 walls far, short dash to cursor is also allowed.)

### Webber

- Whistle: Blow to Herd Spiders, or craft a whistle if don't have one.

### Winona

- Portasol: Equip a Portasol and Activate it
- Catapult: Arm (fast cast)
- Catapult: Barrage (fast cast)
- Catapult: Target (fast/manual cast)
- Catapult: Planar Strike (fast/manual cast)

### Wormwood

- Grow Living Log
- Transform Bulbous Lightbug

### Wurt

### Walter

### Woby
